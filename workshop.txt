title=Crepuscular
description=Crepuscular divides the world clock into day and night and adds special nights and days: moons and suns. Each can change sandbox options, switch The Mutants off and show a line to every player when it begins.
description=
description=Night runs on fixed hours (20:00 to 04:00 by default) or follows the game's own seasonal dusk and dawn.
description=Day and Night each have a definition: sandbox values that change while it lasts (for example ZombieLore.Toughness), whether The Mutants is off, and a line to show when it begins. The values go back to their base when it ends.
description=Events (8 options, each holding any number of them) define moons and suns with their own conditions: moon phase, dates, months, seasons, every N days, days since the world began, every Nth time, right after another event, a cooldown, weather, temperature and chance. An event can start at a set hour and last its night or day, several of them, or a number of hours or days. One event runs at a time, and each night and day is decided once, so a restart never rolls again.
description=The default event is a blood moon: a 50% chance on the first night of each full moon, with The Mutants active all night and a red line on screen.
description=
description=The Mutants (optional): mutants are off at night by default. While they are off, no new mutants appear, and mutants with no target and no player nearby (40 tiles by default) are removed from the server and from every player's game. Zombies already loaded can be offered to The Mutants as soon as mutants come back on.
description=Cry of Fear Monster NPCs (optional): its monsters are never turned into mutants, and the log warns if its Night only option is off. Outfits of other monster mods can be listed so that they are never turned into mutants either.
description=
description=Works in single player and multiplayer, with or without these mods. Admins get a Crepuscular entry in the right-click menu to see the status, force day or night, and start, end or re-decide events. Mistakes in the definitions are logged, and admins see a line about them when they join.
