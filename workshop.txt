title=Crepuscular (testing)
description=[h1]CREPUSCULAR[/h1]
description=[b]Action by day. Horror by night.[/b]
description=
description=[b]In testing:[/b] expect bugs, and settings may still change between updates. Please report problems on [url=https://github.com/zexyqag/Crepuscular/issues]GitHub[/url].
description=
description=Crepuscular gives day and night their own rules: fast, fragile hordes in daylight, slow dead that won't stay down after dark, and special moons and suns in between.
description=
description=[h2]DAY AND NIGHT[/h2]
description=It works as soon as it's enabled, no setup needed. Out of the box a quarter of ordinary zombies run faster by day and all of them are more fragile. At night half of them slow down, half of the night's kills get back up an hour or two later while it's still night, and The Mutants (if installed) stay away.
description=
description=[h2]THE DEAD GET BACK UP[/h2]
description=Killed zombies can rise again where they fell. Burn them, or use Finish off on the corpse, to keep them down.
description=
description=[h2]MOONS AND SUNS[/h2]
description=Create your own special nights and days, by moon phase, date, season, weather, every few days, after another event or at random. Each can change zombie speed and toughness, how many kills rise, most sandbox options, the sky and fog, and which monsters are about, and can announce itself with a line and a sound. Out of the box there's a blood moon: a 50% chance on each full moon of faster zombies, where every kill can get back up.
description=
description=[h2]MONSTER MODS[/h2]
description=Choose when The Mutants, Cry of Fear Monster NPCs and Silent Hill Monster NPCs appear, by day, night or event. Monsters of other mods, such as the Last of Us infected (Zomboid Forge), are removed far from players while they're off.
description=
description=[h2]SET IT UP[/h2]
description=Everything is a sandbox option, on any server. The [url=https://zexyqag.github.io/Crepuscular/builder/]online builder[/url] writes the settings for you, with presets inspired by zombie films and games.
description=
description=[h2]GOOD TO KNOW[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Single player and multiplayer.
description=[*]Every zombie that rises counts as another kill for kill-counting and XP mods.
description=[*]Admins get a Crepuscular entry in the right-click menu: status, force day or night, start or end events.
description=[/list]
