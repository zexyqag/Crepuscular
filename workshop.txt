title=Crepuscular
description=Crepuscular divides the world clock into day and night and adds special nights and days: moons and suns. Each can make a share of the zombies faster or slower, make zombies more fragile or tougher, change sandbox options, switch monster kinds off and show a line to every player and play a sound when it begins, and for a player who joins while it lasts.
description=
description=Night runs on fixed hours (20:00 to 04:00 by default) or follows the game's own seasonal dusk and dawn.
description=Day and Night each have a definition: how fast and how tough zombies are, sandbox values that change while it lasts (for example ZombieLore.Toughness), which monster kinds are off, and a line to show and a game sound to play when it begins or a player joins. The values go back to their base when it ends.
description=
description=Speed: a share of the ordinary zombies moves one or two tiers faster (shambler, fast shambler, sprinter) or slower. The same zombies are picked every time, on the server and on every player's game, and the ones that speed up are the last to slow down. A share of 0 leaves the game's speeds alone. By default a quarter of the zombies are faster by day, half are slower at night, and half are faster on a blood moon.
description=Health: a factor below 1 makes zombies fragile: new and reloaded zombies get that share of their health, and no zombie keeps more than that share of the most a new one can have. Above 1, new and reloaded zombies are tougher. A zombie already around is never healed. By default zombies have 70% of their health by day.
description=Both can grow with the days since the world began: for example from a quarter of the zombies faster to three quarters over the first 60 days, or nights that get a little tougher every day.
description=Monsters of every kind, Bandits and crawlers keep their own speed and are never capped. The health factor is given as the game creates a zombie, before it marks some of them, so a reloaded crawler or a new mutant may get it too.
description=
description=Events (8 options, each holding any number of them) define moons and suns with their own conditions: moon phase, dates, months, seasons, every N days, days since the world began, every Nth time, right after another event, a cooldown, a random wait after each start (for example 3 to 7 days), weather, temperature and chance. An event can start at a set hour and last its night or day, several of them, or a number of hours or days. One event runs at a time, and each night and day is decided once, so a restart never rolls again.
description=The default event is a blood moon: a 50% chance on the first night of each full moon, with half of the zombies faster, The Mutants active all night and a red line on screen.
description=
description=Monster kinds: The Mutants (mutants) and Cry of Fear Monster NPCs (cof) are built in, the Kinds option adds other monster mods by the start of their outfit names, and other mods can register kinds of their own. Each definition lists the kinds that are off; a kind no definition turns off is left alone. While a kind is off, its members with no target and no player nearby (40 tiles by default) are removed from the server and from every player's game. When The Mutants is loaded, members of every kind are never turned into mutants.
description=
description=The Mutants (optional): mutants are off at night by default. While they are off, no new mutants appear and far ones are removed. Zombies already loaded can be offered to The Mutants as soon as mutants come back on.
description=Cry of Fear Monster NPCs (optional): while cof is off, its spawns are set to None (regular and special monsters, Human Flowers and the Sawrunner's night encounters) on the server and every player's game, and far monsters are removed. When cof is on again its own values come back, and, as with its own Night only option, every one of its homes may be filled again, killed monsters' too. For its monsters only at night, add off=cof to Day and keep Cry of Fear's own Night only option off: the log warns if it is on.
description=
description=Other mods can use Crepuscular.API (registerKind, isActive, kindOf, current) and the event OnCrepuscularChange.
description=
description=The Notifications option silences every line and sound on every player's game while the events still run.
description=
description=Works in single player and multiplayer, with or without these mods. Admins get a Crepuscular entry in the right-click menu to see the status, force day or night, and start, end or re-decide events. Mistakes in the definitions are logged, and admins see a line about them when they join.
